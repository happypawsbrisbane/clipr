## Voice

Write with warmth, confidence and restraint. Use Australian English. A small amount of wordplay is welcome, but do not make every sentence a dog pun. Avoid baby talk, generic luxury language and claims that are not supported.

Preferred button labels: Shop the collection, For dogs, For humans, Shop walk gear, See the full set, Add to bag, Meet Good Sort.
