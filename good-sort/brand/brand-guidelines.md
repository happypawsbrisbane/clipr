# GOOD SORT digital brand guide

## Brand idea

GOOD SORT makes considered wearables for dogs and humans. It should feel like a contemporary Australian apparel label that happens to understand dog walks, rather than a conventional pet shop.

Core line: **Made for your kind.**

Descriptor: **Australian wearables for dogs & humans.**

## Logo hierarchy

1. Wordmark: default navigation, storefront and campaign signature.
2. Primary logo: wordmark plus tagline for spacious placements.
3. Secondary stacked logo: square, portrait and packaging placements.
4. GS symbol: avatars, labels, hardware, favicons and small placements.

Clear space should be at least the width of the inner counter of one O. Do not place the full primary logo below 240 px wide digitally. Below that size, use the wordmark or GS symbol.

Never stretch, outline, shadow, bevel, rotate or retype the logo. Do not place it over a visually busy part of a photograph.

## Colour

| Colour | Hex | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oxblood | `#6E2033` | Primary brand, calls to action, core product colour |
| Chalk | `#F5F0E7` | Main background, reversed logo, warm neutral |
| Denim | `#2E4B6B` | Secondary grounding colour |
| Powder | `#B9CFD4` | Soft secondary field and product colour |
| Citron | `#D7E04B` | Small high-energy accents and functional highlights |
| Ink | `#191817` | Body copy, dark surfaces and monochrome mark |

Recommended combinations:

- Oxblood on Chalk
- Chalk on Oxblood
- Chalk on Denim
- Chalk on Ink
- Ink on Powder
- Ink on Citron

Avoid Powder on Chalk and Citron on Chalk for small text because contrast is insufficient.

## Typography

- Display: Neue Montreal Bold. Substitute: Archivo Black.
- Body: Suisse Int'l. Substitute: Inter.
- Labels: uppercase body face with 0.12em to 0.16em tracking.
- Use sentence case for body copy and buttons.
- Let oversized display text do the visual work. Avoid excessive weights and decorative typefaces.

## Photography

Dogs and humans should feel equally important. Choose candid or editorial moments with believable movement, warm natural light and tactile product details. Keep the setting restrained and recognisably Australian through light, materials and vegetation rather than flags, slang or clichés.

Avoid generic pet-stock imagery, tongue-out close-ups, artificial smiles, heavy colour grading and overly cute styling.

## Motion and interface

Motion should be tactile and calm: 180 to 320 ms for interface feedback and longer eased reveals for editorial imagery. Use Citron for small active-state details, not large background areas. Keep corners restrained and avoid turning every section into a rounded card.
